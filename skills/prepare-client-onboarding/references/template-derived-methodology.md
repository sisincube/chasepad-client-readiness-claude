# Methodology derived from ChasePad's templates

The phase-one methodology was checked against two ChasePad workflow assets:

1. **Client Onboarding Checklist for Agencies**
2. **Client Request Readiness Kit for Agencies**

Use their operational detail as a baseline, not as a fixed response template.

## Elements carried forward

- A project snapshot with the client, internal owner, main client contact, final decision-maker, kickoff date, target date, and next action when available.
- Commercial readiness checks covering the signed agreement and required initial payment.
- Separate handling for assets/files, content/copy, access, approvals, and milestones.
- An approval map that identifies what needs approval, who approves it, the method, deadline, and expected turnaround.
- A concise five-to-eight milestone plan that includes client input dates, review, revisions, final approval, and handoff where relevant.
- “Waiting on” logic that retains the owner, status, due point, and next step.
- A decisions-and-notes discipline: preserve confirmed decisions and turn open decisions into specific requests or kickoff agenda items.
- Secure handoff methods for access and files.
- Explicit consideration of the effect of late assets, access, feedback, or approvals on the project plan.

## How the plugin must exceed the templates

The templates are intentionally reusable and manual. The plugin must add value by:

- extracting the relevant facts from the user's actual documents;
- adapting checks to the project type rather than showing every possible item;
- distinguishing confirmed evidence from missing details, ambiguity, recommendations, and assumptions;
- calculating readiness consistently instead of asking the user to choose a colour;
- identifying true start-blockers conservatively;
- assigning practical owners and next actions;
- turning gaps into a tactful, send-ready request;
- retaining requested formats, due points, and secure handoff methods where known;
- avoiding duplication of items already confirmed or not applicable.

Do not copy sample client names, dates, asset lists, or website-specific checks into unrelated projects.
