# Agency project adaptations

Use these checks to make the output project-specific. They supplement the core rubric; they do not replace it.

## Website and product builds

- Confirm page or feature scope, content migration, domains, hosting, CMS, analytics, environments, browser requirements, integrations, technical owners, launch responsibilities, and post-launch support.
- Separate visual assets from page copy and confirm the approved version, format, owner, and source-of-truth location for each.
- Ask for role-based access rather than passwords.
- Treat launch date as conditional on content, access, testing, and approvals when appropriate.

## Brand and design projects

- Confirm discovery participants, existing brand assets, research inputs, deliverable formats, usage contexts, decision criteria, feedback rounds, trademark or legal review, and final file handoff.
- Distinguish creative preference from approval authority.

## UGC, social, and content campaigns

- Confirm campaign objective, audience, platforms, content pillars, hooks, deliverable counts, aspect ratios, raw-footage policy, product logistics, creator usage rights, paid usage, whitelisting, disclosure rules, approval deadlines, and publishing owner.
- Confirm who supplies products, scripts, brand references, music, claims, discount codes, tracking links, and final caption copy, including their due points.
- Do not provide legal conclusions; flag terms that need confirmation or qualified review.

## Video and production

- Confirm treatment, script, cast, locations, call sheets, releases, product availability, shot list, music rights, edit rounds, output formats, captions, delivery channel, and contingency dates.

## Paid media and growth retainers

- Confirm accounts, billing ownership, tracking, pixel or conversion setup, approved claims, creative pipeline, budget, target markets, reporting cadence, performance baseline, and decision thresholds.
- Never ask the client to paste account credentials into chat.

## Ongoing retainers

- Confirm monthly scope, request intake, priority rules, rollover policy, turnaround times, recurring meetings, reporting, capacity boundaries, approval service levels, and change-control process.

## Consulting and strategy

- Confirm the business question, stakeholders, research access, interview participants, confidentiality constraints, decision date, workshop attendance, expected recommendation format, and implementation ownership.

## E-commerce and product launch

- Confirm catalogue or SKU scope, imagery, pricing, inventory, shipping regions, returns, taxes, product claims, platform access, campaign dates, launch dependencies, and customer-support ownership.
